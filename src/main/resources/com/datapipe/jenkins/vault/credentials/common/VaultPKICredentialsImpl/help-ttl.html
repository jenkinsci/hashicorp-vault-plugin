Requested certificate lifetime (e.g. 1h, 30m, 24h). Defaults to 1h.

Best practice: keep the TTL as short as your builds allow. Short-lived certificates minimise the impact of a key compromise and are a core benefit of using the PKI secrets engine over static certificate storage.

Builds share the cached certificate until half of its TTL has passed, so a build is only guaranteed half of the TTL: choose a TTL of at least twice the longest build duration. With Issue a new certificate per build every build gets the full TTL, so the longest build duration plus a small buffer is enough.

The requested TTL must be less than or equal to the PKI role's max_ttl.
